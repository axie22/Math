### R1 — if n² is even then n is even, by contrapositive

Contrapositive statement: If $n$ is odd, $n^2$ is odd

Proof: We claim that if $n$ is odd, then $n^2$ is odd. Since $n$ is odd, $\exists k \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $n = 2k + 1$
Then we have:
$$
\begin{aligned}
n^2 &= (2k + 1)^2 \\
n^2 &= 4k^2 + 4k + 1
\end{aligned}
$$
We know that $k^2$ must be even and that $4*k$ must also be even since 4 is even, therefore $4k^2 + 4k  + 1$ is odd

## Core 1 — unfolding drill

### (a) sum of two evens is even

*Setup:* We assume that there exists two integers $a, b$ such that the sum $a + b$ is even. Let us call the sum, $c$.

*Unfold:* Then by definition $\exists x, y, z \in \mathbb{N} s.t., ~ a = 2x, b = 2y, c = 2z$

*Work:* 
$$
\begin{aligned}
a + b &= c \\
2x + 2y &= 2z \\
2(x + y) &= 2(z)
\end{aligned}
$$

*Land:* Since both sides fall into the definition of even, the sum of two evens is even, 

### (b) product of two odds is odd

*Setup:* Let there be odd integers $a, b, c \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $a * b = c$

*Unfold:* Since $a, b, c$ are odd, there exists $x, y, z \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $a = 2x + 1, b = 2y + 1, c = 2z + 1$

*Work:*
$$
\begin{aligned}
a * b &= c \\
(2x + 1) * (2y + 1) &= (2z + 1) \\
4xy^2 + 2x + 2y + 1 &= 2z + 1
\end{aligned}
$$

*Land:* Since $4xy^2 + 2x + 2y$ evaluates to be an even integer, both sides are odd therefore the result holds true.

### (c) a | b and b | c ⟹ a | c

Setup:

Assume \(a, b, c\) are integers such that $(a \mid b) and (b \mid c)$.

Unfold:

- $(a \mid b)$ means $(b = a \cdot m)$ for some integer (m).
- $(b \mid c$) means $(c = b \cdot n)$ for some integer \(n\).

Work:

Substitute the expression for \(b\) into the equation for \(c\):  
$(c=(a\cdot m)\cdot n)$  
$(c=a\cdot (m\cdot n))$

Land:

Since $(m \cdot n)$ is an integer, $(c = a \cdot k)$ where $(k = m \cdot n$). Therefore, \(a \mid c\)
