## Core 2 — pigeonhole

### (a) Negation of "$f$ is injective"

$\exists a, b$ where $a \neq b$ and $f(a) = f(b)$ 

### (b) No injective $f : \{1,\dots,6\} \to \{1,\dots,5\}$ exists

Setup: Assume, for the sake of contradiction, that there exists an injective function $f : \{1,\dots,6\} \to \{1,\dots,5\}$

Proof: 
The domain has 6 elements but the codomain has 5 elements. By the pigeonhole principle, mapping 6 distinct elements to 5 possible outputs means that at least one output must receive more than one input. Therefore there must exist some $a \neq b, f(a) = f(b)$ which directly contradicts our assumption that the function is injective.
