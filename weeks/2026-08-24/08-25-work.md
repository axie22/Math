True one — proof (construct y from x):
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall x \exists y P(x, y) &= True \\
y &= -x \\
x + y &= 0 \\
x + (-x) &= 0 \\

\end{aligned}
$$

False one — negate it, then exhibit the counterexample:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lnot(\exists y \forall x P(x, y)) &= \forall y \exists x + y \neq 0 \\
&\text{Let x = 1 - y} \\
x + y &\neq 0 \\
(1 - y) + y \neq 0 \\
0 \neq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
We have reached a contradiction therefore this is false
