**(a) A fresh single-set instance.** Prove, from the two-part definition,

$$\inf\left\{\dfrac{2n+1}{n} : n\in\mathbb{N}\right\} = 2.$$

**(b) Sup of a sum — new ground, not a repeat.** Let $A,B\subseteq\mathbb{R}$
be nonempty and bounded above, and define $A+B=\{a+b : a\in A,\ b\in B\}$.

$$\sup(A+B) \le \sup A + \sup B.$$
(Hint: let $s=\sup A$, $t=\sup B$. Show $s+t$ is an upper bound for $A+B$ —
this direction doesn't need the $\varepsilon$-part at all, just the
upper-bound half of each definition, applied to an arbitrary element $a+b$.)

This is deliberately only one direction of Phase 0 exit-gate item 5

which does need the $\varepsilon$-part on both $A$ and $B$ simultaneously, is
